Privacy Policy & Data Governance

Ministry of Earth Sciences (MoES) · Smart India Hackathon 2026 · PS #26078

1. Overview & Statutory Compliance

Project VATAWARAN (AI-Driven Spatio-Temporal Extreme Weather Tracking & Downscaling Engine) is an open scientific research and disaster preparedness platform developed for the Smart India Hackathon 2026 under problem statement #26078. This platform operates in full alignment with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) of the Republic of India and global open data governance standards.

2. What Information We Collect

We believe in radical data minimization:

  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not require account creation, phone numbers, or personal identifying data to access weather bulletins, downscaled fields, or emergency alerts.
  • Zero Third-Party Advertising: No advertising networks, trackers, or commercial pixels are present in this service.
  • Technical System Telemetry: Standard non-identifying server request logs (IP address, user agent, request timestamp, latency) are collected solely for performance monitoring, DDoS mitigation, and rate limiting.
  • Local Browser Storage: We use browser localStorage exclusively to save your UI preferences (e.g. cookie consent, map layer toggles).

3. Meteorological & Government Data Usage

All environmental, meteorological, and disaster management feeds ingested by VATAWARAN (including NCMRWF NEPS-G, IMD AWS, ISRO MOSDAC, and NDMA Sachet) originate from public domain governmental portals and are processed strictly for real-time risk assessment, research, and non-commercial disaster risk reduction.

4. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

VATAWARAN employs only strictly necessary local storage items required for session continuity and interface preferences. No cross-site or persistent user profiling cookies are utilized.

5. Security Architecture

All communication is secured using TLS 1.3 / HTTPS encryption, strict Content Security Policy (CSP), HTTP Strict Transport Security (HSTS), and secure headers. Access to backend administrative endpoints is authenticated and rate-limited.

6. Grievance Redressal & Inquiries

For inquiries regarding data integrity, model transparency, or privacy governance, contact the Project VATAWARAN Core Development Unit at vatawaran.engine@ncmrwf.gov.in.
